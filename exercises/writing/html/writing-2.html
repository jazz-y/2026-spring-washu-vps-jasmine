One thing that brings out the best in me is my dog, Fluffy. Having a dog is kind of like having a child, in a way, but somehow also very different. Whatever mood I am in when I get home, my dog is always happy to see me, looking at me with his soft, brown eyes and wagging his fluffy tail; I can’t stop but smile and pet him and his soft, curly fur as he approaches me eagerly and jumps into my lap for pets and cuddling. When Fluffy lays down next to me on the couch as I work on homework, I love to stroke his soft fur, give him head scratches, and give him belly rubs, inspiring me to keep working. Even at night when he curls up to sleep in my bed, I tend to stay up less because I am more mindful of him getting more sleep. It’s funny that I care more about his sleep schedule than my own. Even when he causes trouble, especially when he was a puppy, I can’t stay angry at him for long; I have become a lot more tolerant to undesirable scenarios and handling them.
