I really cherish making handmade corn tortillas when I’m at home. Anyone can make tortillas, but to master making them you need time and patience. There are so many things that can go wrong; you need to prepare the dough to right the moisture or they’ll tear or be too thick; you need to make the dough balls the right size or they’ll be too small or too big; You need the griddle at the right temperature for the tortillas to puff up like a pita; you need to perfect your technique for laying them on the griddle, or else they’ll break/warp; you need to flip them at the right time so they puff; you need to cook them just right so they’re not raw or burnt; you need to be careful to not burn yourself on the griddle. Surely, you can buy tortillas from the store, but they don’t taste or feel the same. We eat tortillas with every traditional meal when I’m home, often as a replacement for cutlery; good tortillas can really elevate a dish.
