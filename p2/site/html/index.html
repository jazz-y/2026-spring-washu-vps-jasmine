WHO'S WHO IN GREEK MYTHOLOGY

A guide to the characters that inspired Hadestown.

ORPHEUS -Son of the muse of epic poetry, engaged to marry Eurydice. With his golden voice and lyre, Orpheus charmed all living things, even rocks and stones.

EURYDICE -A dryad, or tree nymph, engaged to marry Orpheus. On the day of their wedding, she was bitten by a poisonous snake, died, and descended to the Underworld.

HADES -God of the dead, ruler of the Underworld, sometimes called "the Wealthy One." He fell in love with, abducted, and married Persephone. Her mother, goddess of the harvest, refused to bless the earth while Persephone was below.

PERSEPHONE -Goddess of the seasons, flowers, fruit, and grain. By arrangement, she spent half the year with Hades in the Underworld, causing fall and winter above. The other half she returned to her mother, bringing spring and summer with her.

HERMES -Fleet-footed guide, messenger of the gods, and conductor of souls to the Underworld. In some tellings, it was Hermes who taught Orpheus to play the lyre.

FATES -Three goddesses who together determined the destinies of mortals, measuring their lifespans and apportioning their suffering.

CHORUS -In ancient Greek theater, the Chorus sang, spoke, and danced in unison. Often representing the voice of the citizens, the Chorus commented on the actions of characters and served as an emotional conduit for the audience.

ANAÏS MITCHELL ( Music, Lyrics & Book ) is a Tony and Grammy Award-winning singer-songwriter whose recordings include the original studio album of Hadestown (2010), Young Man in America (2012), Bonny Light Horseman (2019, as Bonny Light Horseman) and Anaïs Mitchell (2022). She was named to the TIME100 list of the Most Influential People in the World in 2020 and her first book Working on a Song: The Lyrics of Hadestown was published by Plume/Penguin the same year. She headlines shows worldwide and her music has featured regularly on "Best-Of" lists, including NPR, The Guardian and The Wall Street Journal. Hadestown is Anaïs's first musical.

Celebrating 40 Years of Fabulous

The Fabulous Fox was built by William Fox and designed by renowned architect C. Howard Crane. Although the opening night movie on January 31, 1929 was a silent film, the Fox was one of the first theaters in the nation with full "talkie" equipment for the Movietone process.

The ornate interior of the Fox has been described as "Siamese Byzantine." One of the crowning features of the Theatre is the 12-foot diameter, 5,280-pound chandelier. It is made of gilded pot metal, enhanced by 2,264 pieces of jeweled glass and illuminated by 259 light bulbs. The St. Louis Fox Theatre is also home to a majestic Wurlitzer organ, one of only five of its type ever constructed.

The theatre was purchased in 1981 by a private partnership that included the late Robert Baudendistel and the late Leon Strauss. In 1981 Fox Associates began a one-year, $2 million restoration program under the direction of Mary Strauss. 7,300 yards of carpeting were woven in the original elephant pattern; missing art glass was authentically reproduced; ceilings were vacuum-cleaned; the chandelier was restored to its original brilliance; and the stage was completely re-equipped with state-of-the art sound, lighting and stage technology.

When the Fox re-opened its doors on September 7, 1982 with the musical "Barnum," the capacity crowd of 4,500 people gave St. Louis' most spectacular showplace a standing ovation. The theatre's historic relevance was recognized when it was accepted into the National Register of Historic Places.

On-going renovation projects have included a $2 million, 20-foot stage addition in 1995; a major lobby renovation in 2000 along with restoration of the ceiling mural; the addition of the Marquee Room and Fox Club for group and corporate dining; the addition of a video marquee in 2005; the installation of a new historically accurate vertical blade sign in 2008; the installation of a $2 million air conditioning system, LED lighting and a new front facade in 2011; the restoration of the magnificent auditorium ceiling in 2013; the opening of Curtain Call Lounge in 2015; and the dedication of our new 600-car garage in 2017.

Since 1982 more than 21 million people have passed through the brass doors of the Fox to see Broadway shows, gospel plays, classic movies, family shows and the finest comedy and concert acts- and to attend tours, seminars, private parties, weddings, graduations and proms.

PEACOCK ALLEY

Visit Peacock Alley on the Fourth Floor to enjoy a spectacular display that highlights the shows and artists presented at the Fox over the past 39 years. When the Fox Theatre originally opened in 1929, Peacock Alley was an art gallery that displayed paintings, statues and objects d'art collected by Eve Leo, wife of William Fox. Now this area proudly showcases the more than 1,800 shows and stars that have appeared at the Fox since 1982; plus, a tribute to Stan Kann, longtime Fox Theatre organist and national TV personality.

About Fox Associates

In 1981 FOX ASSOCIATES was formed by Robert Baudendistel and Leon Strauss to purchase, renovate and operate the Fox Theatre. Today that partnership consists of Mary Strauss, Lisa Baudendistel-Suntrup and Julie Baudendistel-Noonan. This ownership group, in conjunction with the experienced management team, has made the Fox Theatre one of the most respected and well-known presenters of Broadway and other live entertainment in the country. Fox Associates has also successfully expanded to other affiliated entertainment enterprises.

FOX CONCERTS was formed to bring all concert booking and promotion services for the Fox "in-house." Fox Concerts has promoted the biggest names in rock, pop and country music including such artists as Garth Brooks, Aretha Franklin, Janet Jackson, Bruce Springsteen, Sting, Alabama Shakes, Lady Gaga, Jerry Seinfeld, Jack White, John Legend, The Jonas Brothers, Chris Stapleton, Brandi Carlile and many others.

Since 1997, FOX THEATRICALS has become one of the leading commercial theatre production companies in the country. Fox Theatricals has produced 30 Broadway plays, musicals, West End (London) and National Tours, and through its association and leadership of the Independent Presenters Network (IPN), an additional 37 Broadway musicals, National Tours and London Productions. We were honored to bring Fun Home to Broadway in 2015 winning critical praise, 12 Tony nominations and 5 Tony Awards including Best Musical. Our production of Legally Blonde: The Musical opened on Broadway in 2007 and won the 2011 Olivier Award for Best New Musical in London's West End. Additional Tony Awards have been won for The Humans, Red, Thoroughly Modern Millie, One Flew Over the Cuckoo's Nest and Death of a Salesman starring Brian Dennehy. Other Broadway credits include The Prom (2019 Tony Award nomination, Best Musical), Bring It On: The Musical, Everyday Rapture; Caroline, or Change (2007 Olivier Award, Best Musical), The Seagull starring Kristin Scott Thomas and Peter Sarsgaard, Jekyll & Hyde and You're a Good Man, Charlie Brown. Select IPN Productions include Moulin Rouge! The Musical (2020 Tony Award, Best Musical), Matilda (2013 Tony Award nomination, Best Musical), Kinky Boots (2013 Tony Award, Best Musical), Spamalot, The Color Purple (2006 Tony Award nomination, Best Musical and 2016 Tony Award winner, Best Musical Revival) and 9 to 5. Fox Theatricals productions have received 250 Tony Award nominations, winning 79 Tony Awards.

Please Be Fabulous - THEATER ETIQUETTE

  • While COVID-19 is still circulating, WE HIGHLY RECOMMEND YOU KEEP A FACE MASK ON AT ALL TIMES while in the theatre- except when you briefly lower your mask to eat or drink.
  • Please TURN OFF all electronic devices before the show begins. The use of cameras or recording equipment of any kind is strictly prohibited during the show.
